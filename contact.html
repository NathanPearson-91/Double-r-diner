Getting Here

Coming in from Highway 21, take the Falls Avenue exit and follow it south for around a mile. The diner is on your right at the Main Sreet junction, opposite the Sheriff&rsquote;s Department. Look for the sign, you won't miss us!

There is free parking on Main Street outside the diner, and a larger lot behind the building which can be reached from Falls Avenue.
